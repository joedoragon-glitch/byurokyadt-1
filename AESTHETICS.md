# V9 construction reference

The cinematic office render and propaganda poster supplied by Joel govern the machine silhouette: narrow left switch bank, near-square recessed CRT, ministry plate at upper right, two meters and confirmation button across the waist, projecting input bed, open output press, softened cabinet corners, and side handles/vents.

The cabinet uses real layered geometry, not a flattened image of the reference. Worn enamel is an AI-generated base-color texture; labels, meter scales, CRT, and documents are drawn deterministically so the text stays correct. Source texture: `assets/enamel-v9.webp`, built-in image generation. Prompt: seamless flat warm gray-green institutional enamel, fine grain, restrained scratches and chips, neutral diffuse illumination, no text, emblems, borders, shadows or hardware.

Audio is original synthesized material, with no third-party music or archival samples. `scripts/build_audio.py` produces a sparse 64-second minor-key instrumental, room hum, and mechanical effects. Controls independently mute machinery and music. No sound starts before a user action.
